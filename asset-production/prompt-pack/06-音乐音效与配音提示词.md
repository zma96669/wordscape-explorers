# 音乐、音效与英语配音提示词

音乐、音效、英语语音分开生成。当前提供的是提示词和录制脚本，不包含实际音频。

## M01 营地循环音乐

```text
Compose an original 60-second instrumental loop for a cozy exploration-and-collection learning game for children aged approximately 9–12. Warm, curious and gently playful; around 84 BPM in a simple major key. Use a soft marimba, a few felt-piano notes, restrained pizzicato strings and a very light shaker. Keep the arrangement sparse and dynamics even, with space for spoken English. No vocals, no speech, no dramatic build, no intense bass, no sudden loud sounds, no copyrighted melody or imitation of a named artist. Aim for a clean loop with compatible beginning and ending; provide a dry WAV master if supported, with no clipping. The loop seam must be checked and edited after generation.
```

建议名称：`music-camp.wav`。60秒是目标长度，工具不支持时导出后剪辑。

## M02 探索循环音乐

```text
Compose an original 60-second instrumental loop for a calm English word-exploration puzzle game for children aged approximately 9–12. Curious and lightly adventurous, around 96 BPM, with a soft plucked melody, quiet wooden percussion and a gentle background pad. Keep it less melodic and less busy than a main theme so it does not interfere with listening tasks or reading. No vocals, no speech, no time-pressure feeling, no ticking clock, no tension rise, no jump scares, no recognizable existing melody. Keep dynamics even and leave headroom. Aim for a seamless loop and provide WAV if supported; the final seam will be checked and edited.
```

建议名称：`music-exploration.wav`。播放语音时音乐应由游戏降低音量，不在配乐文件中混入语音。

## T01 统一英语配音风格

在支持独立风格栏的语音工具中，将下面内容放入风格栏；实际朗读文本从CSV的text列读取。选择同一个声线ID，后续批次保持不变。没有风格栏时只提交正文，不把提示词一起朗读。

```text
Use one consistent, friendly adult voice with clear General American English pronunciation. This is educational audio for children aged approximately 9–12. Speak naturally and calmly, slightly slower than normal conversation, without exaggerated baby talk or theatrical character voices. Pronounce each word accurately; do not spell it unless the supplied text explicitly asks for spelling. Read only the supplied text, exactly once, without an introduction, translation, title, explanation, music or sound effects. Keep volume and timbre consistent across files. Preserve punctuation pauses. For cloze-task text, pronounce the supplied word “blank” instead of guessing or revealing the missing answer. Do not read file names, IDs or pronunciation notes aloud.
```

英语口音暂用en-US，是可替换的制作默认值，不代表公司已指定该口音。

## 录制脚本

使用`content/voice-script.csv`中的156行分别生成音频。每行都给出了正文、用途、中文核对含义、目标路径和发音提示。

- 单词48条。
- 含义及语境任务72条。
- 收藏名称和短句36条。
- 听音找词任务复用对应单词音频，不必重复录制。

特别核对：read在本词包读现在时/riːd/；close为动词/kloʊz/；T. rex按tee reks读；三角龙、剑龙、翼龙等名称需逐条试听。收藏名称中的兴趣词不自动加入常规学习测试。

## 音频交付检查

音频和CSV一一对应；去除无意义的长静音但保留完整起音与尾音；无削波、无异常响度跳变；人工试听英文发音、句子自然度和空缺提示。保留原始音频，运行时MP3与WAV源文件分开。不能只检查“文件存在”就标记听审通过。
